Privacy Policy

App: FamyLoop
Developer: FamyLoop
Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

FamyLoop is a personal family care app designed to help individuals manage health check-ins, medications, to-do lists, and brain training exercises. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. TERMS&CONDITIONS

FamyLoop does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with external servers. All data entered into the app (check-ins, medications, to-do items, game progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

3. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally using Hive, a lightweight local database. No data leaves your device. No account or login is required to use the app.

4. Permissions

FamyLoop requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send medication and check-in reminders.
  • Microphone: Used only for the Voice To-Do feature (speech-to-text). Audio is processed on-device and never recorded or stored.
  • Exact Alarm: To schedule precise medication reminders.

5. Third-Party Services

FamyLoop does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

FamyLoop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
